    Coach on its own

    The game only shares your character when it saves (a /reload, logging out, or the "Save for the coach" key), so this happens right after a save, never live. What is worth knowing is worked out here from what changed since the last save, for free: a level-up, nearly full bags, a finished quest, a death, unspent talent points, worn gear, a new zone.

    The advice choice uses one call of your plan per save, at most.

    Saved.

    What goes where

    • When the coach thinks, these things go to OpenAI, through your own ChatGPT sign-in, and only then:
      • your character's data from the game (the report of its latest save),
      • your question,
      • the last few messages of the conversation,
      • the coach's notes: your own notes and the character journal (you can turn this off in Settings, "Send the coach's notes to the AI").
    • Nobody else gets it. wow-coach has no server and no telemetry.
    • Everything else stays on this computer: your settings, your usage counts (only numbers, never your questions), and the answers in the game's addon folder.
    • Other players' names are hidden from the AI unless you turn that off in Settings.
    • This window only works on this computer. No other computer can open it.
    • Full details: the privacy notice.
